      <article>
        <h3>1 · Free Space Path Loss</h3>
        <code>FSPL[dB] = 32.44 + 20log10(fMHz) + 20log10(dkm)</code>
        <p id="fsplLive">d=180 m, f=3500 MHz → FSPL≈88.4 dB.</p>
      </article>

      <article>
        <h3>2 · Log-distance Model</h3>
        <code>PL[dB] = PL(d0) + 10nlog10(d/d0) + Xσ</code>
        <p id="plLive">n=3.2, d=180 m → PL≈115.5 dB.</p>
      </article>

      <article>
        <h3>3 · Vegetation / Clutter</h3>
        <code>Lveg[dB] = K · fGHz^0.3 · dveg^0.6 · ρ</code>
        <p id="vegLive">dveg=28 m, density=0.68 → Lveg≈3.1 dB.</p>
      </article>

      <article>
        <h3>4 · Received Power</h3>
        <code>Prx[dBm] = EIRP + Gsector - PL - Lveg - Lshadow - Lfade</code>
        <p id="prxLive">EIRP=46 dBm, Gsector=7.2 dB → Prx≈-71.1 dBm.</p>
      </article>
