      <section class="rf86-doctrine">
        <article>
          <h2>1 · Free Space Path Loss</h2>
          <code>FSPL[dB] = 32.44 + 20log10(fMHz) + 20log10(dkm)</code>
          <p id="rf86FsplText">d=185 m, f=3500 MHz → FSPL≈88.7 dB.</p>
        </article>
        <article>
          <h2>2 · Log-distance Model</h2>
          <code>PL[dB] = PL(d0) + 10nlog10(d/d0) + Xσ</code>
          <p>n controlla la severità ambientale. Non è un effetto grafico.</p>
        </article>
        <article>
          <h2>3 · Vegetation / Clutter</h2>
          <code>Lveg[dB] = K · fGHz^0.3 · depth^0.6 · ρ</code>
          <p id="rf86VegText">Lveg≈2.7 dB, depth=28 m, clutter controllato.</p>
        </article>
        <article>
          <h2>4 · Received Power</h2>
          <code>Prx[dBm] = EIRP + Gsector - PL - Lveg - Lshadow - Lfade</code>
          <p id="rf86PrxText">Prx≈-61.3 dBm, SINR≈20.7 dB, best-server=SITE-B.</p>
        </article>
